Footage Farm Vimeo access

Some official Footage Farm screeners require a Vimeo sign-in. You can let yt-dlp use a signed-in Edge or Chrome profile, or choose an exported Netscape-format cookies file. Jewish Reels stores only the selected method, profile name, or file path; it does not copy cookie contents.

No Vimeo sign-in configured.

Close the selected browser before preparation if Windows prevents yt-dlp from reading its cookie database. Cookies are used only for verified videos on the official Footage Farm Vimeo account.

About the review

Large sheets are split into overlapping regions up to 1,280 pixels, preserving the original detail. Every region is reviewed. Disagreements are logged and treated as unsure; they do not become hits.

The run budget pauses before the next request once recorded spend reaches your limit. Requests already in flight can exceed it: at most one in Take turns mode, or up to the selected worker count in Run concurrently mode. Provider rate limits trigger a shared cooldown and up to six retries; unfinished regions never become no verdicts. Session spend includes requests in this workspace since this app opened; estimates are labeled. Total spend includes recorded costs across all sessions in this workspace, including retries and unfinished reviews. It is not the OpenRouter account balance. Average cost per video is recorded total workspace spend divided by completed video IDs across all sessions, including hits, no hits, reused IDs and existing verdicts. Billed retries and unfinished work contribute to spend; unknown charges are excluded. It is unavailable until one video is complete. Videos per minute is the session average of completed video IDs, including shared-reel copies, divided by active review time; pauses and time closed are excluded.

Results go to chat_verdicts.json; progress goes to logs/inspect_live.json. Existing verdicts are preserved. Optional titles, URLs and shared MP4 paths come from reelsight_manifest.json.

What counts as a hit.

Turn cues on or off, edit the wording sent to the model, or add your own. Save before starting review. Unfinished videos restart when the saved rules change; completed verdicts stay as they are.
